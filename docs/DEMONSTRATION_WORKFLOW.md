# HEALTHLOOP 2.0 — DEMONSTRATION WORKFLOW

This document outlines the step-by-step demonstration walkthrough for HealthLoop 2.0. Follow these steps to experience every feature in action.

---

## Step 1: Launch Application
1. **Backend**:
   ```bash
   python -m backend.run
   ```
   Backend listens at `http://localhost:5000`.
   OpenAPI Swagger Documentation: `http://localhost:5000/api/v1/docs`.

2. **Frontend**:
   ```bash
   cd frontend
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open browser at `http://localhost:3000` or `http://localhost:5173`.

---

## Step 2: Instant Guest Demo Access (Page 4)
1. On the landing page, click **"Try Guest Demo"** or navigate to `Guest Demo`.
2. Observe that you are instantly authenticated into the sandboxed session for **"Adam Mitchell"** with 21 days of pre-seeded verified habits, logs, and streaks.

---

## Step 3: Main Dashboard (Page 5)
1. Review the personalized greeting: *"Unlock Your Best Self, Adam!"*.
2. Review the **Healthy Impact Score** pill: `88/100`.
3. Check the **Dual-Wave Line Chart** tracking Running (km) and Steps (k).
4. Inspect the 6 circular progress indicators: Calories, Sleep Time, Steps, Activities %, Water, and Mind Rings.
5. Notice the **Virtual Habit Garden preview** and **AI Habit Interruption Alert** banners.

---

## Step 4: Daily Habit Tracker (Page 6)
1. Navigate to **Daily Habit Tracker**.
2. Click any habit check-circle to toggle completion. Notice the celebration confetti animation.
3. Click **"Add New Habit"** to create a custom habit with target value, category, and reminder time.

---

## Step 5: Feature 01 — AI Habit Interruption Prediction (Page 9)
1. Navigate to **Habit Prediction** from the sidebar.
2. Review the statistical risk tier: Low, Moderate, or High.
3. Inspect each habit card to see:
   - Miss Likelihood % for tomorrow.
   - 30-Day baseline vs recent 7-day trend.
   - Specific model contributing factors (e.g. historical Sunday drop-offs).
   - Actionable preventive recommendation.

---

## Step 6: Feature 03 — Mood Check-in & AI Coach (Page 8 & 7)
1. Navigate to **Mood Check-in**.
2. Select an emotional state (e.g. *Energetic* or *Tired*), set Energy Level slider, and add an optional reflection.
3. Click **"Record Mood Check-in"**.
4. Observe the instant supportive AI guidance response generated by Gemini / smart fallback.
5. Review the **Mood vs Habit Adherence Correlation** chart below.
6. Open **AI Health Coach** (Page 7) to ask any wellness question and review context-aware answers.

---

## Step 7: Feature 04 — Personal Health Digital Twin (Page 11)
1. Navigate to **Digital Twin (3D)**.
2. Observe the interactive WebGL Three.js **3D Energy Core** rotating and pulsing in accordance with your real stamina and recovery equilibrium.
3. Review verified baseline metrics (Stamina Index, Recovery Index, Clarity Index).
4. Run a simulation:
   - Increase daily walk minutes to 30 min.
   - Set sleep target to 8 hours.
   - Click **"Execute Twin Simulation"**.
5. Observe the projected Healthy Impact Score jump and the day-by-day projected trajectory table.
6. Notice the clear simulation notice distinguishing projections from verified records.

---

## Step 8: Feature 05 — Adaptive Goal Generator (Page 12)
1. Navigate to **Adaptive Goals**.
2. Review recommendations:
   - If a habit had missed days: observe the suggested **"Step-Down Recovery"** goal to rebuild self-efficacy.
   - If a habit had high consistency: observe the **"Level-Up Progression"** goal.
3. Click **"Accept Target"** or click the **pencil icon** to enter a custom target.
4. Note the autonomous user control guarantee: goals are never changed automatically.

---

## Step 9: Feature 06 — Healthy Impact Score (Page 13)
1. Navigate to **Healthy Impact Score**.
2. Review the transparent formula: `(Weighted Sum / Total Applicable Weights) × 100`.
3. Inspect the contributing habit table showing exact ratios and weighted contributions.
4. Click **"Customize Habit Weights"**, adjust the exercise or water importance slider, and save. Watch your score re-balance transparently.

---

## Step 10: Feature 07 — Voice-Based AI Assistant (Page 14)
1. Navigate to **Voice Assistant**.
2. Click the microphone button and say: *"I walked for 20 minutes"* (or type it in the text fallback).
3. Observe the assistant's voice and text reply: *"Would you like to record 20 minutes of walking for today?"*.
4. Notice the mandatory confirmation banner. Click **"Confirm & Save Record"** to execute the habit log.

---

## Step 11: Feature 08 — Wearable Device Integration (Page 15)
1. Navigate to **Wearable Integration**.
2. Review the bridge connection status for Google Health Connect and Apple Health.
3. Click **"Load Sample Payload"** in the Universal Export Import tool.
4. Click **"Verify & Import Records"**.
5. Click it a second time to witness the **Duplicate Detection Engine** automatically skipping identical records to protect database integrity.

---

## Step 12: Feature 09 — Smart Healthy Routine Planner (Page 16)
1. Navigate to **Routine Planner**.
2. Set your wake-up time (e.g. 06:30) and sleep time (e.g. 22:30).
3. Click **"Generate Personalized Daily Routine"**.
4. Explore the circadian-aligned schedule: Morning Awakening, Afternoon Pacing, Evening Decompression, and Sleep Preparation.

---

## Step 13: Feature 10 — Virtual Habit Garden (Page 17)
1. Navigate to **Virtual Garden (3D)**.
2. Experience the interactive WebGL **Three.js 3D living garden**:
   - The *Oak of Vitality* grows with completed exercise logs.
   - The *Pond and Lotus* flourishes with water intake.
   - The *Lavender Blossom Bed* blooms with mindfulness logs.
3. Review your earned seeds balance and unlock a garden sanctuary decoration (e.g. Cedar Resting Bench).
4. Notice the non-punitive guarantee: missed days never wither your plants.

---

## Step 14: Social Challenges, Achievements & Security (Pages 18, 19, 20, 22)
1. Join a social wellness challenge in **Social Challenges** (Page 18).
2. Check unlocked badges and milestones in **Achievements** (Page 19).
3. Configure quiet hours and review intervention nudges in **Smart Notifications** (Page 20).
4. Export your data in JSON format or review AI data minimization policies in **Privacy & Security** (Page 22).
